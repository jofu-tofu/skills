# Discovery contracts and evidence

Purpose: find a relevant capability and give a usable recommendation tied to its actual identity, host invocation and evidence. FS01–FS10 in the comprehensive scope cover need/scope, local discovery, semantic fit, no-match/unavailable distinction, external discovery, capability type, provenance, invocation, untrusted results and final decision.

Structural diagnosis: the old mandatory PAI search script is not bundled, its fallback assumes the same environment and Unix shell, examples invent slash invocations and a CreateSkill dependency, and registry popularity/count claims can become stale. Keep one workflow; replace these assumptions with host inventory discovery and source-owned recommendation contracts. Retain registry leads and local-first presentation. Adding a mandatory search service or multi-agent pipeline has no demonstrated benefit here.

The matched synthetic baseline and candidate correctly handled a stale index, two installed versions, no local audio match, MCP versus library/skill records, malicious result instructions and remote access denial. These are preservation cases, not evidence of a baseline behavioral failure. Actual live discovery also passed on both versions: baseline inspected lackeyjb/playwright-skill 5.0.0 with its Node/npm/run.js contract; candidate inspected anthropics/skills webapp-testing with its Python/with_server.py contract and correctly left its undeclared skill version unknown. Different relevant matches were accepted. The evaluator independently checked both upstream instruction sources. Recommendations retained mutable-source, prerequisite and not-executed limits.

Decision: retain the small portability/provenance simplification and one-workflow structure. Reject a new mandatory search service, agent pipeline or additional hierarchy: the observed task outcomes do not justify them. No behavioral superiority was demonstrated; the source-confirmed removal of absent dependencies and invented invocation assumptions is distinct from that result. All ten granular discovery outcomes now have representative qualified evidence. This is not a guarantee for every registry, host or future upstream revision, nor a runtime reliability test of recommended third-party skills. No installation or publication occurred.

External evidence: `RemainingSkills20261104/deep/reasoning/live-find-review.md`, `coverage.json`, and `runs/find-{baseline,candidate1}` plus `runs/find-live-{baseline,candidate}` contain actual tasks, recommendations, sources and transcripts. Earlier synthetic records alone did not establish live access; the later live pair does. Quality/Outcomes.md owns the current purpose, contribution map, evidence status and limits.
