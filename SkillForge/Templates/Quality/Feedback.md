# Feedback template

Status: template, not a populated quality record.

Record: Observations, reference evidence, judge, qualification checks, limits, uncertainty handling, and revision triggers.

For every new, changed or unsupported measure, use [DesignMeasurement](../../Procedures/DesignMeasurement.md) and link a populated [Measurement specification](Measurement.md), or include its fields here. Record evidence-method alternatives, provenance/independence, exact scoring, qualification status and scope. An incident is not required. Reused measures need an explicit applicability check; designs awaiting qualification cannot certify improvement/readiness.

Link relevant gap IDs, outcome IDs, measure/checker/case-set versions and qualification evidence. Record whether a reported gap requires new cases, a changed measure, a clarified outcome, or a skill repair; use [DiagnoseGap](../../Procedures/DiagnoseGap.md). Preserve old results when measurement changes.

Include scope, version/date, sources, unresolved questions, and revision triggers. Reuse existing authoritative records by link. Keep reserved tests outside routine skill context.

Handoff: [PlanChange](../../Procedures/PlanChange.md) for creation/update, or EvaluateCandidate for assessment-only work.
