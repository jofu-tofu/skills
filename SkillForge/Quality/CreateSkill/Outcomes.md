# Outcomes — CreateSkill

These proposed requirements specialize [Shared](../Shared.md); do not score document count or wording similarity as usefulness.

- **C01:** The candidate addresses the stated need without inventing mandatory requirements.
- **C02:** Instructions and entry points lead to the intended behavior on representative tasks.
- **C03:** The assessment uses a declared reference or no-skill baseline where the claim requires one.
- **C04:** Delivery distinguishes a usable draft from demonstrated readiness.

Origin: workflow outcome and shared commitments, operationalized here as draft checks. Validate these checks before using them as authoritative references. Revisit when they miss failures or reject valid alternatives.
