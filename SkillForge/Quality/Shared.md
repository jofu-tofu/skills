# Shared quality requirements

Proposed v0.1, 2026-11-03. Source: the project quality discussion and Axioms and Foundations commitments D01–D03. These are proposed operational rules, not proven optimal choices.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| S01 | Identify intended outcome, audience, requirement sources, and unresolved assumptions. |
| S02 | Preserve versions, observations, uncertainty, and reasons behind decisions. |
| S03 | Keep protected requirements explicit; aggregate gains cannot conceal mandatory regressions. |
| S04 | Freeze comparison criteria before interpreting results; separately justify later changes. |
| S05 | Match claims to evidence scope and distinguish development cases from fresh assessment. |
| S06 | Bound work by the authorized scope and declared budget; allow justified no-change decisions. |
| S07 | Reuse applicable criteria and revise obsolete ones with reasons and authority. |
| S08 | New, changed or unsupported measures require DesignMeasurement; improvement/readiness claims require applicable qualification evidence, not merely a score or judge agreement. |
| S09 | Preserve pass/fail/unverified separately: missing evidence blocks confirmation without proving failure. Keep instrument qualification separate from target performance. |

Cost and practical benefit thresholds depend on the task. Structural compliance is not behavioral effectiveness. At the method level, compare useful gains against a simpler process under comparable budgets.
