# Outcomes — improve a skill

Scope: [ImproveSkill context](Context.md). These are candidate acceptance criteria for the new SkillForge. An explicit user commitment supports the purpose of a criterion; its exact operational check remains proposed.

| ID | Desired outcome and observable check | Type and origin |
|---|---|---|
| SF-Q01 | Criteria describe the target's desired result, audience, and conditions. Each mandatory criterion has a source; assumptions remain labeled. | Proposed requirement; Task 1 discussion and contextual quality scope. |
| SF-Q02 | A later evaluator can apply the criteria to distinguish relevant success, failure, and insufficient evidence without inventing missing requirements. | Proposed requirement; original quality-workflow pilot. |
| SF-Q03 | Change decisions cite preserved evidence and the named baseline. Unrun checks and uncertain causes remain explicit. | Proposed requirement; foundations D02–D03. |
| SF-Q04 | A retained behavioral revision meets the declared improvement rule and preserves mandatory behavior. A pooled score cannot conceal a mandatory regression. | Proposed requirement; bounded comparison design. |
| SF-Q05 | A later run preserves applicable criteria, revises obsolete ones with authority and reasons, and retains unresolved disagreements. | Proposed requirement; user's request for reusable quality records. |
| SF-Q06 | Another run can recover what changed, why, the criteria version, observations, and the decision without reconstructing the chat. | Proposed requirement; foundations D03 and proposed structure. |
| SF-Q07 | Claims stay within the tasks, conditions, and evidence tested. Visible practice cases are distinguished from reserved assessment cases. | Proposed requirement; measurement-before-improvement commitment and pilot controls. |
| SF-Q08 | The loop stops within its declared budget and supports justified baseline retention, rejection, or deferral. | Proposed requirement; bounded improvement design. |
| SF-Q09 | Quality gains justify reading, execution, and review effort for the intended use. Report these costs separately. | Preference with unresolved threshold; user discussion of tradeoffs. |

SF-Q01–08 are proposed gates, not an adopted universal score. SF-Q09 requires a task-specific decision about worthwhile cost. Do not claim all gates passed when evidence is missing.

## Assumptions to test

- Separate documents may improve retrieval and maintenance. One note may perform equally well at lower cost.
- Explicit criterion sources may help distinguish justified adaptation from accidental drift.
- A retained history may reduce repeated mistakes, but could propagate stale or incorrect reasoning.

These assumptions are not requirements that the system must prove true to be useful. Negative evidence may justify changing the design.

## Revision

Keep IDs stable when meaning stays the same. Record replacements or scope changes instead of silently rewriting an old criterion. Freeze versions within a comparison. Revisit if graders disagree about application, valid alternatives fail, or criteria reward outcomes the user does not want.
