# Outcomes — EvaluateSkill

These proposed requirements specialize [Shared](../Shared.md); do not score document count or wording similarity as usefulness.

- **E01:** The assessment distinguishes success, failure, and insufficient evidence under stated criteria.
- **E02:** Checks detect relevant faults and accept valid alternatives.
- **E03:** Evidence identifies target version, conditions, cases, judges, and limitations.
- **E04:** Evaluation leaves the target unchanged; readiness judgments are separately identified.
- **E05:** Reported gaps and suspected measurement failures are diagnosed before freezing evaluation, with proposed changes and unresolved evidence kept in the assessment workspace.

Origin: workflow outcome and shared commitments, operationalized here as draft checks. Validate these checks before using them as authoritative references. Revisit when they miss failures or reject valid alternatives.
