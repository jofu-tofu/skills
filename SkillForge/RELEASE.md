Current draft: 2.1.0-rc.9. Broad improvement now owns comprehensive parent outcome coverage and baseline structural review independently of successive experiment rounds. Matched completion checks improve from 29/34 to 34/34. Two actual improvement comparisons tie on independent outcomes; proportionality and corrected adequate-skill controls pass. Retained for bounded completion protections, with no general behavioral superiority established. Deeper target application remains in progress; no installation or publication is implied. See [current record](Improvements/ComprehensiveImprovement/Record.md).

## Prior rc.8 scope

Version 2.1.0-rc.8 added conditional research planning and separate candidate/task decisions. Its [completion-gate record](Improvements/CompletionGate/Record.md) preserves prior tests and live checks. Earlier installation authority does not install this draft.

## Prior rc.7 scope

Version: 2.1.0-rc.7. Adds outcome decomposition, contribution mapping, experiment-adequacy checks and explicit continuation into outcome evaluation. Ordinary improvements use a small complete decision loop; larger studies require a decision and evidence that justify their cost. The final decision gate checks scope assumptions and composition across handoffs, returning feasible required checks to evaluation rather than closing prematurely. Narrow maintenance remains lightweight. The [experiment record](Improvements/ExperimentAdequacy/Record.md) preserves 29 completed trials, ties, rc.5/rc.6 failures and rc.7's successful exact replay and preservation control. This is bounded engineering evidence, not universal autonomous reliability or a rerun of all prior release qualification.

The purpose-first walkthrough produced owner-agreed MarkdownToDocx outcomes and a bounded six-example check trial. This supports development use, not superiority over the prior method or autonomous reliability. See the [change record](Improvements/PurposeFirst/Proposal.md). Publishing this candidate does not itself install it or establish broader effectiveness.

Integrates the retained v0.7–v0.9 evidence-preservation, regression-coverage and conditional-dependency methods with the portable research library from 2.0. Native discovery remains unverified; explicit workflow use is evaluated separately. Read [validation scope](Evidence/ValidationSummary.md).

## Supported entry points

CreateSkill, UpdateSkill and EvaluateSkill retain design-only, lightweight, cases-only and assessment-only boundaries. Their procedures and useful research references are bundled. Historical external research identifiers are provenance text, not required paths.

## Tools

Supported structural validation is limited to the bundled legacy format. The benchmark aggregator is qualified for the tested metric units/missingness/counts contract; see [format contract](Tools/BenchmarkFormat.md). Legacy description evaluation has bounded offline Windows stream/consumer coverage, not qualified native discovery. Optional viewer and exploration helpers remain experimental; they are not required to complete core workflows. See [tool status](Tools/README.md).
