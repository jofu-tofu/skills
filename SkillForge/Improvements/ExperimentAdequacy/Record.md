# Experiment adequacy and evaluation continuation

Date: 2026-11-04. Retained candidate: 2.1.0-rc.7, for bounded engineering use. Owner authorized autonomous improvement iterations and model trials in disposable workspaces; human review checkpoints were withdrawn. Installation and publication remain separate.

## Observed problems and mechanisms

The preceding outcome-mapping change stopped after instruction edits and static checks. A subsequent twelve-run rc.2/rc.3 comparison generated proposals while explicitly forbidding testing and explicitly requesting contribution mapping. Both versions produced useful mappings. It could not establish autonomous discovery of those responsibilities or continuation through evaluation. Human judging remained unqualified. Preserve this null result rather than claim that the change improved outcomes.

Relevant experiment-design advice already existed in optional research material. The selected workflow lacked a clear operational check that a proposed test could settle the user's decision, preserve the behavior being tested, and reach the affected final outcome. Merely adding a checklist also risked excessive research planning.

Rc.4 added PlanChange's experiment-adequacy check, ConstructCases decision coverage and U07, plus explicit autonomous continuation. It requires credible alternatives, baseline opportunity, realistic subject information, consequential breadth, reference qualification, useful next actions and matched claim scope. U06 retains the outcome-evaluation requirement added in rc.3. Explicit design-only and lightweight maintenance scopes remain distinct.

## Initial executed comparison

Twelve fresh model subjects compared complete frozen rc.3/rc.4 packages under matched settings. Four planning subjects used CodeReview/Research context. Eight live subjects covered receipt repair, rejection of a harmful simplification, correction of a faulty checker while preserving the skill, and optional dependency applicability.

All twelve completed. Command traces confirmed actual outcome checks and decisions in the eight live tasks. External probes, withheld from subjects and qualified against correct implementations, valid alternatives and known faults, passed 42/42 across those eight results. Both versions repaired the actual faults and retained correct behavior in the opposing cases. No method files changed. This establishes bounded execution observations, not a comparative advantage or population reliability.

The plans reached relevant end outcomes, but over-specified large later studies. CodeReview plans were 3,040 words in rc.3 and 3,231 in rc.4; rc.3 budgeted up to 232 invocations, while rc.4 proposed 96 confirmation reviews alone. Rc.4 Research proposed 60 diagnostic plus 96 confirmation executions. Conditional staging mitigated this, but the work and numerical margins preceded evidence of the actual failure or required precision.

## Second revision and assessment

Rc.5 makes the next experiment a small complete decision loop: reproduce a consequential failure where applicable, contrast valid behavior, follow the affected handoff to its final result, and decide repair/no-change. It commits only the next decision's resources, leaves larger sampling conditional, and discourages invented precision thresholds and redundant records. This does not authorize silently narrowing a broad reliability claim.

Eight follow-up trials completed across four fresh paired tasks: MarkdownToDocx/handoff experiment planning, a correct helper behind a faulty invoice-report consumer, and a harmful helper proposal behind an apparently working consumer. Final process output, rather than helper-only checks, was the integration outcome. All 24 predeclared final-command probes passed. Both versions rejected harmful helper changes and performed actual evaluation. Planning outputs showed a useful change in commitment: rc.5 started with three diagnostic conversions or three writer/receiver loops rather than budgeting large future studies. This is unblinded qualitative evidence from two cases, not a general effect estimate.

Two additional complete-context rc.5 checks reused existing quality/portability records and did not confuse prior constructed examples with actual skill execution. The richer document-conversion context led to a larger diagnostic plan, so minimal plan size is not a consistent or sufficient success claim. These were exposed development questions, not new independent holdouts.

Subjects discovered a large safe-integer rendering error omitted by the reference. Exact integer quotient/remainder independently confirmed it. The reference amendment and additional exposed-development probes were preserved; both versions passed all twelve additional single-value probes. Do not penalize a valid new finding because it was absent from the key.

Four paired duration/CSV tasks completed with 24/24 external checks, but its exact original failure replay still passed only 8/9: the aggregation counterexample remained wrong. Related task success did not establish resolution of the actual failure. Rc.5 and rc.6 are not the final retained candidate.

## Final gate revision and decision

Rc.7 moves the contract/scope check into ApproveSkill and explicitly routes feasible missing checks back through UpdateSkill to evaluation. The gate examines whether guarantees for individual valid inputs survive the affected composition, and distinguishes retaining a partial gain from completing the user's requested outcome. It does not demand unrelated fixes or exhaustive proof.

Two unchanged-request development trials completed: the original failing invoice task passed all 9 external checks, including exact aggregation; the CSV preservation control passed all 5 checks. Command traces show actual baseline/candidate evaluations and final decisions. No subject changed its supplied method files. Retain rc.7 as the bounded tested candidate; do not infer universal or population superiority. One successful exact replay cannot estimate repeat reliability.

Across the 29 model trials, all executions completed. Canonical results preserve two failed outcome observations (rc.5 and rc.6), not just the final success. Planning judgments remain unverified for independent confirmation, with concrete qualitative observations retained. The initial partial-context designs and later complete-context designs are identified separately. Richer context can legitimately expand case coverage; neither fewer cases nor shorter prose is the quality objective.

Final package checks: 14/14 structural checks, 236 resolved local Markdown links and clean whitespace. Operational instructions match the tested rc.7 snapshot; subsequent changes are informational release/evidence/index summaries. These checks describe the pre-publication draft. The owner subsequently authorized publication and installation; Git history records the published revision.

Preflight qualification caught a generated CSV escape error before model runs. Before scoring CSV outputs, the checker was corrected to accept equivalent JSON key ordering; qualification rejected wrong fields/counts while accepting valid order variants. These setup corrections and the large-number reference amendment are recorded rather than silently relabeled as target results.
