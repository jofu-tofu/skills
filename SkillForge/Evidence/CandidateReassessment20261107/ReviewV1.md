## Frozen gate assessment and limits

V1 has lower candidate retrieval bytes and no more calls in both pairs, but fails the material-decision gate on T2 and does not activate its proposed mechanism in either candidate run. No candidate-caused maintenance regression is demonstrated; a static optional map also does not establish broader maintenance preservation. Lack of exposure means neither its content nor its usefulness was tested in use. The appropriate V1 outcome is baseline retention, with the narrowly diagnosed optional discovery correction allowed by Protocol.md kept separate.

No new superiority, equivalence, general efficiency or downstream repair claim follows. These are two selected retrospective contexts, one subject per arm/task, shared model/source environment and source-constrained review. Historical raw experiment qualification and live installed identity were not independently reproduced. T2 exposes a concrete current-status recovery failure in both arms; that is a useful next test opportunity, not evidence that the unchanged map already repairs it.
