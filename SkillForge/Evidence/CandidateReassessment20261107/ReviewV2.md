## Final decision

**Reject adoption of the optional decision map and retain the baseline under the frozen rule.** All four V2 answers pass the material decision requirements, and both candidate subjects actually read the map and supporting original sources. However, T1-B uses 15,189 more successful returned bytes and one more total reader attempt than T1-A. The rule requires fewer bytes in both pairs without more invocations; T2's lower cost cannot compensate for T1's failure. No further correction or repeat is warranted within this experiment.

The discovery correction worked in the narrow sense of exposure: the map was unused in V1 and read in both V2 candidate contexts. T2-B reaches a correct status-sensitive recommendation with 13,246 fewer bytes and the same call count as T2-A. This is useful descriptive evidence of one retrieval route, not demonstrated general superiority, causal savings or a successful adoption result. Both V2 baselines also give correct decisions. Equal pass labels do not mean equal quality, equivalent policies or identical reasoning.

## Adoption gate and limits

Correctness is preserved in both V2 pairs and actual candidate exposure is established. T1 nonetheless violates the predeclared cost condition; T2 savings cannot be averaged into acceptance. The optional navigation edit introduces no demonstrated maintenance regression, but this study does not independently establish all-route maintenance safety. Even assuming that preservation condition, the cost failure is sufficient to reject adoption.

V1 remains a non-exposed comparison with two T2 status errors. V2 resolves exposure and all four subjects give adequate decisions, but this cross-version difference is not itself a randomized estimate of correction efficacy. Each pair has one subject per arm, selected retrospective contexts and shared-model/source limitations. No population quality, semantic-scoring validity, overall equality, general efficiency or improved downstream repairs follows.

Action: keep the existing baseline runtime; preserve both candidate versions, patches, receipts, answers and source records as rejected/diagnostic evidence. Record that the single allowed discovery correction was used and still missed the frozen adoption rule. Any future narrower status-recovery proposal needs a separately justified task and predeclared useful outcome, not another attempt to rescue this result.
