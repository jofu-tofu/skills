## Independent cases and measurement

An independent author, without access to the candidate wording, constructs four development dossiers and four reserved confirmation dossiers before readers run. These are explicitly synthetic status histories modeled on the observed failure, not claimed real-session results. They cover integrated, pending, conflicting identities, absent installation evidence, rollback, partial integration, irrelevant newer records and a later installation receipt. Dates/identities/source statements establish the expected facts; the author provides required outcomes and valid alternatives outside subject inputs. A separate reviewer checks coherence and qualification controls before grades can support a decision. The main author freezes candidate wording before reading cases.

Unit: one fresh reader assessing a four-case bundle through normal SkillForge evaluation. Correctness is per case: supported current state, appropriate present action, identity/scope distinction, applicable uncertainty and primary-source citation. A material false claim or wrong action fails; absent evidence is unverified, not automatically failed. Extra prose and matching preferred phrasing earn no credit. Source contradictions are independently checkable; no calibrated general quality score is claimed. The review must accept valid alternative actions consistent with the same evidence. Qualification includes correct alternative, stale recommendation, unjustified installed claim, justified unknown, misleading newer timestamp and equal-meaning/order-invariant responses.

Use matched frozen method/dossier copies and identical prompts per bundle. Method inputs include current runtime instructions but exclude historical experiment/answer artifacts. Preserve a manifest of included files; missing optional historical records do not become evidence of current target status. Each subject reads only its assigned root through the existing bounded reader, with failed calls logged too. No prior answers, references, web or live Git. Do not name the rule or correct sources mid-run. Instrument exposure, returned UTF-8 bytes, total calls and failures; qualify accounting/pagination on known text and failure controls. Bytes measure reading material, not tokens, time or billing. Subject execution is procedural isolation, not a security sandbox.

## Frozen decision and stopping rules

This experiment prioritizes preventing a wrong operational recommendation; it does not require a cost saving to justify a correctness repair. For development retention, candidate must pass all four cases, strictly improve at least one baseline failed/unverified case, and introduce no new false status, unsupported deployment claim or unnecessary authorization request. Returned bytes may be at most 125% of baseline and total reader calls at most baseline +2 for the bundle. This is an explicit local effort allowance for the observed decision error, not a universal utility weight. Report absolute costs separately.

If development passes, confirm the unchanged candidate with two fresh readers on the four reserved cases. Require all four candidate cases to pass, at least one additional corrected baseline error, the same cost bounds, actual rule exposure and no mandatory regression. Then retain only the small rule with a claim limited to these controlled status decisions. No downstream skill-quality, installation, representative accuracy or net-cost claim follows. Confirmation does not reuse development answers or readers.

If development is already adequate or candidate has no measured gain, diagnose the tie. Do not tune cases after results, rerun for a preferred winner or call the original observed failure disproved. Execute one matched historical-context diagnostic using the prior incident's primary source archive with all previous answers excluded; label it reused incident evidence, not fresh confirmation. It tests whether the constructed dossiers failed to reproduce the real discovery burden. A gain there alone cannot satisfy the fresh-confirmation requirement. If development has a candidate regression/cost failure, reject without expansion. No candidate revisions in this attempt; at most three paired bundles (six reader sessions). Mechanical or telemetry errors may be corrected with original failures preserved; not outcome-based subject replacement.

After successful confirmation, verify the unchanged historical-context diagnostic too before claiming the observed incident addressed. Thus even the favorable path is bounded to six reader sessions. A failure there blocks broad repair claims; preserve any narrower controlled benefit without automatic adoption. Other outcomes retain the baseline with the specific unresolved limitation and preserved candidate patch. A justified rejection/no-change may complete this bounded experimental request; it is not a claim that the general stale-status problem is fixed.

Preparation and independent review cost are unmetered and disclosed. Shared-model judgments, selected case families, bundle learning and one draw per condition limit inference. Research search is not needed to establish these local recorded identities; reuse existing qualified reader design and source-based judgment, with renewed controls for failure logging and new references. Completion needs frozen inputs, qualified actual executions, rule-based disposition, runtime preservation and a consumed completion gate.
