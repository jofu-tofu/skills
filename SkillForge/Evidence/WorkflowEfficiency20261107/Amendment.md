# Follow-up decision — frozen before execution

V1's reuse mechanism is not demonstrated: both Ledger arms repeat reads, and the
Prompting candidate also rereads PlanChange and DraftCandidate. Preserve the
started Prompting run and its planned baseline counterpart as whole-packet
cost/preservation observations only. Stop attributing costs to successful reuse.
Do not repeat V1 or claim it reduces duplicate reads.

Ledger identifies a more concrete avoidable operation: the candidate loaded five
quality templates plus result/decision redirect files and their destinations,
then wrote its own compact records. The baseline also loaded extensive templates.
These layouts mostly repeat fields already specified by the procedures; they are
not executable outcomes. V1's generic reuse paragraph did not prevent loading.

Follow-up V2 replaces V1's paragraph with explicit optional prose-template loading
for focused Update attempts. Required procedure/standard rules and machine-readable
schemas, qualification, completion format and gate remain required. A template is
loaded when needed to fill a missing record field or an actual consumer requires
that format. No removal of outcome evidence, checks or raw observations.

Smallest next contrast: one Ledger V2 author with the same exact prompt/fixture,
compared descriptively with the preserved V1 and baseline. This is an adaptive
follow-up on exposed development material, not fresh confirmation. No new checker
or thresholds; inspect actual template loads first. If it still loads unnecessary
templates or costs do not improve while quality is preserved, reject and retain
baseline; do not keep tuning prose. If it shows a material coherent gain, run the
same real Prompting family for transfer/preservation before retaining V2. Typo
control applies to whichever version is considered for retention. No population
or causal speed claim from these small unmatched-order observations.

For the V2 proceed decision, require preserved external outcomes and complete,
truthful evaluation plus fewer commands, lower reported input/output tokens and
lower author elapsed than the existing Ledger baseline. This conservative
all-dimensions rule avoids choosing a favorable cost unit after seeing V2; it is
a local experiment allocation rule, not a universal cost utility or effect-size
claim. If any required counter is unavailable, do not claim the rule passed.
