## Changes

- ReadabilityGate freezes actual round inputs; Verify reconciles artifact and audience/context identity. Creation, review and conversion share a final-delivery check. Valid evidence survives relocation or unrelated errors; changed dependencies reopen affected checks.
- PlainRewrite checks meaning again when paragraph/connective repair changes already accepted wording. Rejected or unchecked wording falls back to applicable accepted/original text.
- Connective repair precedes the next frozen snapshot. Contract results update the authoritative working contract without changing the old snapshot.
- Repair review explicitly reassesses unresolved earlier Blockers/Majors using current evidence. Declining an unsafe edit does not resolve its harm. A clean new lens or answers to other questions cannot erase it. Fixed, inapplicable and wrongly diagnosed findings can still be closed with evidence.

The last change arose during this evaluation: both baseline X and candidate v1 lost a previously identified novice-use gap after unrelated repairs. The v1 runtime and every actual report remain preserved. The amendment was independently reviewed, then tested through the affected Verify consumer on the actual completed Y artifact/evidence.
