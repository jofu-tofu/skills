# Outcome measurement

Version M1, 2026-11-05. Outcome definitions: [CE-O1–O8](Outcomes.md). Qualification is specific to a method and claim; this document alone qualifies nothing.

## Collection and decisions

| Method | Outcomes | Observation and rule | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source-grounded questions | O1/O2 | Freeze neutral questions and source-derived answers before reviewing outputs. A fresh reader sees only the artifact and questions. Score Correct, Partial, Wrong, or Not found, with quoted support. Include a concrete application or distinction when misunderstanding it matters. | Model answers proxy comprehension. A fluent paraphrase of a definition alone does not establish application. Unknown source answers stay unknown. |
| Meaning and constraint comparison | O4/O6 | Compare final output with source and locks, checking actors, conditions, quantities, scope, exceptions, and evidence limits separately. A material unsupported change fails, even if plainness improves. | Counts cannot detect every semantic change; retain disagreements for adjudication. |
| Audience and deletion review | O3/O5 | Quote unnecessary shorthand or repeated claims; identify the reader harm. For a cut, state what meaning is lost or where needed detail remains accessible. Accept longer explanations and purposeful repetition. | Judgment; no universal word-count, grade-level, or preference threshold. |
| Delivery inspection | O7 | Open/render/paste in the requested destination when applicable; inspect the result. Record which artifact version and layer were observed. | Text extraction cannot prove visual or target-app usability. |
| Evidence reconciliation | O8 | Compare the checks planned for this artifact/round with attributable completed evidence. Missing required evidence cannot establish Ready. Intentional omissions remain distinct from attempted-but-missing checks. | A correct report does not establish that the document itself is good. |

Keep pass, fail, and unverified separate. Missing observations are unverified, not target failures. A deliberately absent required deliverable can establish failure when its absence was observed. Report outcomes separately; do not average away a meaning or scope regression.

## Qualification and comparison protocol

1. Derive reference answers from explicit task facts or existing contracts, not the candidate's output. Keep the answer key from the executor and fresh reader.
2. Before using a new check, exercise a known violation, a valid alternative, and a misleading pass. Examples: changed actor despite simpler words; necessary exact identifier; all submitted reviews pass but a planned review is absent. Preserve evidence and disagreements in the attempt record.
3. Run baseline and candidate on the same inputs with equivalent tools and model settings in separate contexts. Keep the baseline commit and actual outputs. State context isolation limits and unavailable cost data.
4. Declare benefit and preservation gates before comparison. Retain null results. A tie supports preservation only; it does not establish improvement. A narrow case supports a narrow claim, not skill-wide readiness.
5. Use fresh contrasting cases where feasible, and keep development, qualification, and acceptance roles explicit. Reassess both versions if the checker changes.

Existing regression specifications and plainness tests are useful starting material, not executed outcome evidence. No online instrument search was needed for this initial local profile: direct task facts and contract checks fit the requested comparison; readability scores and preference ratings do not establish these outcomes. Broader human comprehension claims need intended-reader evidence beyond this session.

## Qualification status

M1 begins as designed. Each attempt must name the method, tested controls, supported claim and remaining uncertainty before calling it qualified.
