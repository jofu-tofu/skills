# Personal Writing Style

Write like a knowledgeable colleague explaining the work: plain, direct, and connected. Simplify the wording, not the reasoning. Apply this voice to ClarityEngine's own instructions and to everything it produces. Follow explicit audience and template requirements, and preserve quotations, required terms, and source meaning.

The voice has three parts. Each part's rules, tests, and examples live in one lens file, so the author and the reviewer read the same rule.

| Part | What it means | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain** | Familiar words, named subjects, concrete verbs, and no jargon the reader does not need | `Lenses/AudienceFit.md` (RC-2, RC-20, RC-29) |
| **Direct** | The point first, each passage earning its place, and the design standing on its own without conversation history or commentary | `Lenses/Navigator.md` (RC-5, RC-26), `Lenses/Cutter.md` (RC-16) |
| **Connected** | Every step linked to the next with "because," "so," or "when," and claims kept accurate when simplified | `Lenses/Logician.md` (RC-13, RC-25) |

When the parts pull against each other, keep the reasoning. A shorter sentence that drops the "because" is not plainer; it is less connected.
